# Store listing (English)

**App name**: Word Relay Battle

**Short description (80)**: Type a word for the category using a letter nobody has used yet. Play friends live!

**Full description**
Type a word that fits the category and starts with a letter nobody has used. Run out of time, or reuse a letter or word, and you're out. Last one standing wins!

Modes
- Solo vs the AI
- Pass and play on one phone (2-4 players)
- Online rooms with a KakaoTalk invite (2-4 players, real time)

Features
- Korean (initial consonants) and English (A-Z)
- Voice input with tappable suggestions
- Words missing from the dictionary only get a warning; players settle it with a challenge vote
- Bright, playful design and sound effects
- No sign-up
